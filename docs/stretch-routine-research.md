# Stretch routine research

Backs `LLR/Logic/StretchRoutines.swift` (post-workout cool-downs for push, pull and legs sessions). Researched 2026-10-13.

**Limits of this note, stated up front.** The primary papers and publisher pages could not be fetched from the environment this was written in (outbound access to pubmed, aafp.org and frontiersin.org was blocked). Everything below comes from search-result summaries. Quotes are marked UNVERIFIED until someone checks them against the primary text; the dosing in the app does not depend on any single quoted sentence.

## Sources

1. **ACSM position stand, 1998 (flexibility recommendations), as summarised by the American Family Physician note "ACSM Revises Guidelines for Exercise to Maintain Fitness"**, Am Fam Physician 1999;59(2):473. https://www.aafp.org/pubs/afp/issues/1999/0115/p473.html
   - Summary says flexibility work 2–3 days a week, at least four repetitions per muscle group.
   - UNVERIFIED quote (as returned by search): "Static stretches should be held for 10 to 30 seconds."
2. **Garber CE et al., "Quantity and quality of exercise for developing and maintaining cardiorespiratory, musculoskeletal, and neuromotor fitness in apparently healthy adults", Med Sci Sports Exerc 2011** (ACSM position stand; supersedes the 1998 one). Secondary summaries attribute to it: each major muscle-tendon group on at least 2 days a week, holds of 10–30 s, 2–4 repetitions. UNVERIFIED against the stand itself.
3. **Behm DG et al., "Acute effects of muscle stretching on physical performance, range of motion, and injury incidence in healthy active individuals: a systematic review", Appl Physiol Nutr Metab 2016;41(1):1–11.** Reported (abstract-level, via secondary citation): small-to-moderate performance decrease (about −3.7%) when testing immediately after static stretching.
4. **A 2019 review co-authored by Behm, Frontiers in Physiology** (https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fphys.2019.01468/full), summarised in search results as: short static stretching (≤60 s) has trivial negative effects on strength and power, prolonged stretching (>60 s) more; and, citing Blazevich et al. 2018 and Reid et al. 2018, short static stretching inside a full warm-up did not impair later strength or power. UNVERIFIED wording.

## What the app does with this

| Choice | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Hold per stretch | 30 s | Top of the 10–30 s range in sources 1–2 |
| Per side | one 30 s set per side (two sets) | Keeps total per muscle at 60 s or less (sources 3–4) |
| Timing | after the session, as a separate workout | Source 3 concerns stretching *before* performance; a cool-down avoids that issue entirely |
| Selection | 4–5 stretches for the muscles that category loads | Practical scope for a 5-minute cool-down; not taken from a source |

Exercise text is original (no copied descriptions or images).
